# Prior Art and Patent Landscape

Analysis of existing patent disclosures, expired public-domain mechanisms, and technology whitespace relevant to rotational omnidirectional solar collectors.

## 1. Key Patent Classifications

- **IPC F24S 23/70**: Solar heat collectors with optical concentrators using reflectors.
- **IPC F24S 23/30**: Solar heat collectors with optical concentrators using lenses or refracting elements.
- **IPC H02S 40/22**: PV components with optical elements for concentrating sunlight.
- **IPC G02B 19/00**: Non-imaging optical systems and condensing lenses/mirrors.

## 3. Public Domain Baseline

The following architectural concepts are firmly in the public domain and free of patent infringement:
1. Revolved 3D Compound Parabolic Concentrators (circular 3D CPCs).
2. Cylindrical involute reflectors combined with compound parabolic upper sections (Winston-Rabl involute).
3. Pure dielectric solids of revolution utilizing Snell refraction at the aperture and TIR on sidewalls (classical DTIRC).

## 4. Whitespace Opportunities for RoSoCol

Patent whitespace exists primarily in tailored geometry and multi-physics integration:
1. **Annular Toroidal Revolved Optics**: Non-standard toroidal profiles specifically optimized for an elevation acceptance corridor ($\theta \in [25^\circ, 65^\circ]$) that minimize skew-ray turning caustics on a central vertical cylinder.
2. **Integrated Fluid-Photovoltaic Core**: Specialized mechanical coupling between a flexible back-contact PV ribbon and an internal coaxial counter-flow thermal heat pipe housed inside a stationary rotational non-imaging optic.
3. **Additive Freeform Dielectrics**: 3D printed graded-index or micro-faceted rotationally symmetric dielectric caps designed for passive all-weather daylight harvesting.
